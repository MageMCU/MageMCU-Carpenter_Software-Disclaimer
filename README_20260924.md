## Students and Young Builders
All children and teenagers: talk with your parent, guardian, mentor, or teacher before experimenting with any of these electronic circuits. Never work with electricity alone. **Electricity Kills.** See the notice below.

***NOTICE***: Keep flammable materials, liquids, and loose metal objects away from power supplies and powered circuit boards. Use a power supply with an enclosed case to protect minors. When instructing young students, keep power sources out of reach and follow a hands off approach while circuits are powered. Horseplay is not permitted and is grounds for removal from the activity.

Additional hazards common to microcontroller and robotics projects:
- **Batteries:** Lithium polymer (LiPo) and lithium ion batteries can overheat, swell, or catch fire if shorted, punctured, or charged improperly. Use the correct charger and never leave a charging battery unattended.
- **Short circuits:** A shorted battery or supply can make wires and components hot enough to burn skin.
- **Soldering:** Soldering irons reach temperatures that cause burns. Work in a ventilated area and use a proper iron stand.
- **Moving parts:** Motors, wheels, and gears can pinch fingers. Disconnect power before handling a robot's drive system.
- **Eye protection:** Wear safety glasses when soldering or trimming component leads.
